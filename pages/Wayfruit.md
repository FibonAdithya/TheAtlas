*I throw the apple at you, and if you are willing to walk with me, take it and share your journey with me; but if your thoughts are what I pray they are not, even then take it, and consider how short is the path.*

Also known as Zman's tithe, the wayfruit or path apple is a custom shared amongst travellers. A token (often fruit or food) is offered to others as a gift of protection and by accepting the two agree to share the road. 
Wherever the reciever rests, they plant the apple's seeds as a homage to [[Zman]] and a sign that they are keeping the road for those who come after them. 
The tradition is thought to have originated in the [[Scorched Sea]] where growing apples is quite difficult and as such sharing them was a patronage of sorts to other travellers.

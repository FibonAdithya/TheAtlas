The campfire encounter is a very simple system that shows the characters growing, deeping their bonds and coming to realisations about the world.
First the gamemaster asks one player a question of their choice, for example:
- "What do you think about ..."
- "How has .... changed over this adventure"
- "What's a crazy story that you've heard?"
- "..." - Backstory question
That player then asks another player a question, and play continues as such until all players have gone.
Once all players have gone, the last player to go may ask a question to the gamemaster who will answer truthfully (an omitted answer should be reasked).

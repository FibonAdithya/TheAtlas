The aim of travel is to make it feel like a journey has happened. Between Points A and B. Typically in fiction this comes down to 3 parts,
1) Preperation Phase: The group charts the path, discusses tradeoffs,  and foreshadow challenges
2) The Journey: Through a series of encounters, we get the impression of time passing and challenges being overcome (the journey is always hard)
3) The Arrival: The group summit the peak, turns the corner of the road/ warp into the planet. Their destination is in view, but something has changed leading to a humerous or dramatic cliffhangar

We emulate this using the travel subsystem:
1) Preperation Phase: 
	1) First several encounters are preseeded, these are default "bad" encounters that are relevant to the plot or area
	2) Players now submit their own enocunters, in pairs of one good and one bad (roughly equally weighted). This is the party charting paths and hearing rumours of what may lie on the journey
2) The Journey: We roll on the random table and run those encounters
3) The Arrival: We have a campfire encounter (see [[Campfire Encounter]]) and have the group arrive at their destination.

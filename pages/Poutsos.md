In the lands of [[Vor'ak]] there is a cactus known as Poutsos. From this plant skilled alchemists extract and create Prickling Pins and Puss. These are both used by villagers and the [[King's Dark Lanterns]] for the detection and deletion of mages. The Prickling Pin is a spine of the cactus that will cannot pierce a veil, so it draws no blood from those that are magically inclined. The Prickling Puss acts as a coolant, suppressing fire and flames and so is used to limit the damage done by the [[Firepox]] plague.

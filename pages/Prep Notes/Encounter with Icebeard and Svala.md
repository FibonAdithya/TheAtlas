### Premise
The players have been meddling a bit and have attracted the attention of the morozkin and osvald.

To warn them away from meddling and get them to return to town Icebeard and Svala have been sent to deliver the message.

The encounter takes place at a bridge. Party walk in on Icebeard killing a rimepack runner. Svala spots the group and sends Icebeard to negotiate. Icebeard crosses the bridge and tries to negotiate with the group. On that side of the bridge there is also zombies hiding in snow mounds.

### Background
Icebeard is the chief of the snowblin tribe. He found the [[Refrykt Helm]] and used it to stop the [[Rime Pack]] from hunting and raiding the [[Snowblins]]. 

He is being controlled by the Morozkin through a rune in his back, but removing it will kill him. 

### Encounter Design
We want Icebeard to be a fierce viking warrior (think like chief), he will cross the bridge and threaten the party to leave. A small part of him wants the party to continue so that they can free the tribe and stop the [[Morozkin]]. We want this conflict to be visible, but as soon as combat starts his eyes glaze and he goes into kill mode.

We want to showcase changing weapons to take advantage of weaknesses so we'll plant some zombies hiding in the snow that Svala can command "Rise and kill them".

This sets up a nice ambush like scenario which should keep the group busy while Svala flees. Of course they expect to easily dispatch the party but it likely will not be so easy.

We want to show Icebeard is a threat but on the level of a werewolf (plus his magic items) so we'll aim for CR 3. Then 140XP gives us CR 3 (80 XP) +  3 x CR -1 (20XP).

Now let's stat out icebeard. He is a warrior chief so we'll base him on the level 3 warrior template with a few magic items. We will take a few features from the fighter class to make it easy to make him feel fearsome.

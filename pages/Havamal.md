*"Fire is needed by the one who has come in, chilled to the knee; food and clothing are needed by the man who has journeyed over the mountains."* 
Havamal (or the right of warmth) is a custom practiced in [[Yor]]. Predating the [[Aratin Empire]], Havamal is the custom of sharing campfires and warmth even amongst strangers and enemies. There are many stories for why the custom exists, though two are by far the most popular.
- [[Zman]] walks the road ahead of you and keeps it safe. Those who follow his example by tending to other travellers will find their journey easier.
- [[Villsvin]] the ..., inviting him to the campfire will keep him from attacking your group. 
The invitation is given verbally, with no token or similar exchanged

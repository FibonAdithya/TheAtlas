Saint (Santka) Leonid is a living saint and a (former) member of the [[Santka Luinaire]]. Originally born in [[M'angas]], Leonid was a devoted acolyte of the Luinaire. During his time there he served as a librarian researching the past saints and especially ones that had been forgotten. He began to believe that the Light was much older than the Luinaire believed and not only had there been Saints before [[Santka Alina]] but that the list of Saints was carefully curated where each could serve a purpose for the Light. 

Because of his blaphemous beliefs, Leonid was excommunicated from the Luinaire and banished from M'angas. Following his studies, Leonid saw that there was a gap in the pantheon of saints and that he himself could fulfil that role before [[Santka Illamder]]. Determined to prove the Luinaire wrong he travelled the world presenting himself as the Saint of Comfort, tending to the sick and needy. At some point during his travel he has ended up on the [[Candle Coast]] though those who follow him call themselves the [[Palliators]].

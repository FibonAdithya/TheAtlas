*I leant upon a village gate
            When Frost was spectre-gray,
And Winter’s dregs made desolate
             The weakening eye of day.
The tangled traplines, slackened, lie
             Like strings of broken lyres,
And all mankind that haunted nigh
             Had sought their household fires.*

Talvitafl (Snow and Tooth) is a board game played in [[Yor]]. The game is composed of a square board (carved of wood, stone or bone) and several pieces (divided into Families and Snow) and a bag of tokens (teeth and pebbles). There are two players in the game - "Winter" and "Village" - with traditionally the host taking the role of Winter. 

The center square is the fire, and play begins with Village setting up their pieces representing the Families around the fire. Winter then sets up their pieces representing Snow along the edge of the board. Players alternate taking turns to move one of their pieces. Snow pieces can capture Families by standing on either side of them. At the end of the Village turn they draw from the bag a number of tokens equal to the number of Families still remaining. The aim of the game is for the Village player to draw all the teeth in the bag before Winter captures all their Families.

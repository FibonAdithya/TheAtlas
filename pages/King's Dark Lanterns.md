Levels of the Kings Dark Lanterns:
- Wick: Sworn but unlit. Carries a lamp but can't open it. Takes denunciations, manages crowds, holds doors etc.
- Lantern: The knight proper. Shuttered to a single slit. Walks the bridges, makes arrests, gives witness. This is the rank people mean when they say the Lanterns came.
- Shutter: The investigator. Decides which denunciations are opened and which are ignored. Half-open lamp.
- Kindler: Pronounces the purification and lights it. Open flame, always. Witch burner.
- Lord Lantern: Head of the order. Answers to the [[Crown of Stars]]

Smack ships are a type of fishing vessel, manufactered in [[Grarjord]] and [[Wyran Principalities]]. The trawllers come with a 'well', a hole in a room at the bottom coated in refined [[Elusium]], this allows them to preserve their catch for much longer or to keep their catch growing and breeding. Because of their small size allowing them to be fast and manuverable many smack ships are used by smugglers to run lines and transport cargo. Since the [[Treaty of the Crown]] many nations have also retrofitted warships into smack ships, though no doubt they could quickly be changed back.

Ancient traditions give the Shaman the power to channel Gods (Or at least god kings). Rather then the [[Tol Leyemil|rote]] taught in other parts of the continent, a shaman connects their very being to the [[God-Kings|God-king]] they draw power from. They are most commonly found in [[Yor]]. The training to become a shaman often takes at least a decade and takes a grueling toll on the mind, soul and body of the shaman. The life of a shaman only ends in doom, with the god king slowly extracting their price until the shaman can pay no more. Because of this they are regarded with fear and suspicion and their help is only reluctantly accepted as everyone knows that at any moment the shaman may bring ruin to friend and foe alike.

*A long time ago, in ages past, the Frost Father walked across Yor. Where he stood, the winter would not end. The snow grew thicker, the storms were heavier, the land could not sustain growth. The wolf mother ran with her pack. As prey fell short their bellies grew thin and they began to waste. Soon they turned to scavenging, fetching bodies that were frozen beneath the snow - but those were not theirs to eat. They ate the dead bones and all.*
*One day the Frost Father caught her. He did not kill her, but he had to show his domain was his and his alone. He held her jaw open and day after day he pulled tooth after tooth. For 42 days she suffered and once defanged, he let her go, let the snow take her.*
*And so the wolf mother roamed, withering for there was nothing she could eat without her teeth. She found a pot and started a fire and boiled the bones till she could drink. This was the first fire. People saw the fire and came to it. They brought their dead, crack the bones that she could not and fed her. In return she would lend them help, labour to toil in the weather that they could not, fires to gather round and keep the cold at bay.*
*She grew strong on the pot. When she was strong enough the other godkings came north, and together they put the Frost Father under the ice, and spring came once more.*
*She never got her teeth back. Ask where they went and every house says something different. Perhaps he swallowed them. Perhaps he threw them in the snow and the white wolves grew where they fell, which is why they do her biting. Perhaps she has them at the bottom of the pot and is waiting for them to go soft.*

The teeth of the [[Wolf Mother]] are a fabled relic that comes from ages past. There are said to be 42 teeth in the set, though no doubt many more have been found. Their exact properties seem to vary by region, with some granting protection from the snow and others giving comand over wolves.

**In a sentence.** What the party walks into.

**Needs.** What the scene requires to work (snow, a shelter, dusk, an NPC travelling with them)

## Encounter

What happens, in the order it happens. Who is there and what they want.

**Ways out.** How it ends without a fight, and when the other side gives up.

**Creatures.** Stat blocks, count, XP for four players. How to scale for more or fewer.

**Hazards and checks.** Traps, weather, group challenges, with DCs.

## Clues

**Free.** What the party learns with no roll, with its meaning. 

**Bonus.** What a check adds. 

**Leads.** Which direction it points, not which place.

## Treasure

**Treasure.** Coin, goods, items

**Strings.** What taking it, returning it or leaving it changes, and who notices.

## Aftermath

What is different in the world depending on how it went: fought, talked, avoided, ignored.

## Sessions

Where it was used and what happened.
